Sharing a free tool I've been using to break out of flying the same routes — SimLetsFly (simletsfly.com) Works with MSFS 2020, MSFS 2024, and X-Plane 12. You set your criteria — distance range, region of the world, airport size, runway length, ILS requirement, heading direction — and it randomly picks a flight that matches. Hit the button again to get a different one, or lock the departure and randomize only the arrival. Results include: - Full flight brief with live METAR/TAF weather - Interactive route map - Vertical profile with TOC/TOD and live winds aloft - Direct export to MSFS 2020, MSFS 2024, or X-Plane 12 Free account gets you a personal logbook where you can rate flights by category (takeoff, landing, scenery, ATC, fun factor), add pilot notes, mark flights completed, and track overall stats. No install, works in any browser. Originally a Windows desktop app (LetsFly), now rewritten from the ground up as a web app.
