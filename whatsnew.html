CheckRide — Grade Your Flights
NEW
CheckRide is a free Windows companion app that monitors your X-Plane 12 flight from departure to parking brake and grades your performance — speed discipline, landing quality, G-forces, bank angles, and system checks. After each flight it generates an AI-written debrief specific to your penalties and how you compared to your last run. Results attach to your SimLetsFly flight cards so you can track improvement over time. Think of it as gamification for your sim flying, built around the same standards a real CFI would apply.
Free during beta · X-Plane 12 · Windows 10/11 · Learn more →
Add Manual Flight to Logbook
NEW
My Flights now has an + Add Manual Flight button. Enter a departure and arrival ICAO — the airport database autocompletes as you type, filling in the airport name, coordinates, and calculating distance and bearing automatically. Optionally set a date flown, mark it completed, and add pilot notes. The new entry drops straight into your logbook and opens expanded so you can add ratings right away.
Load Route from Logbook
NEW
Every card in My Flights has a Load Route button. It sends the flight back to the main app with departure, arrival, cruise speed, and pilot notes all pre-filled — including the full IFR waypoints if the flight was saved with a route. Works with manually added flights too.
Vertical Profile Tab
NEW
A third tab has been added to the results panel alongside Flight Details and Map View. It shows an SVG altitude profile chart for your route — climb to TOC, cruise segment, and descent to TOD — along with a stats bar covering cruise altitude, cruise speed, descent rate, and phase times (climb / cruise / descent).
The tab also includes a Winds Aloft card (sign-in required) that fetches live wind speed, direction, and temperature at your cruise altitude from Open-Meteo. It calculates headwind or tailwind component and shows an adjusted flight time with the delta versus no-wind time.
Cruise altitude and speed can be overridden in the profile tab without affecting your main cruise speed setting.
Last Flight as Departure
NEW
A new departure option in My Flights lets you use the arrival airport of your most recently saved flight as your next departure. Great for chaining a realistic trip — you land somewhere, and the next flight picks up right where you left off. Shows airport type, city, and runway length so you know what you're working with.
HTML Flight Brief Export
NEW
The Flight Brief menu now includes Save as HTML Brief — a formatted HTML document you can open in any browser. It includes a live route map, two-column departure and arrival cards with runways and METAR/TAF, flight summary, vertical profile chart, winds aloft data, and pilot notes.
The route map requires an internet connection to load tiles. All other data is fully embedded in the file.
Flight Brief Now Includes Vertical Profile
UPDATE
Both the clipboard copy and text file export now include a Vertical Profile section with cruise altitude, cruise speed, descent rate, TOC, TOD, cruise segment, and phase times. If winds aloft data is loaded, an adjusted flight time and wind component are appended as well.
